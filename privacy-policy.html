Privacy Policy for Teep

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Teep ("the App") is a local Wi-Fi remote control application for smart TVs and monitors. The App operates entirely on your device and your local Wi-Fi network.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, analytics, device identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry on external servers.

2. Local Network Communication & On-Device Storage

  • Local Wi-Fi Discovery & Control: When you scan for displays or press remote buttons, Teep communicates directly over your local Wi-Fi network between your phone/tablet and your TV or monitor. None of this traffic passes through any external server.
  • On-Device Storage: Saved display IP addresses, friendly names, optional Wake-on-LAN MAC addresses, and local TV pairing tokens/keys are stored strictly on your device so you stay paired across sessions. You can delete saved displays inside the App or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Services, Ads & Tracking

Teep contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking libraries. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Teep does not collect personal data from users of any age, including children under 13.

5. Independent Application Disclaimer

Teep is an independent universal remote application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of Sony Group Corporation or Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. Brand names are referenced solely to indicate compatibility.

6. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the developer contact email listed on the store page.
